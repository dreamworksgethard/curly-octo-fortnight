// about Chaineye AI

The next-gen intelligence layer for Base

Chaineye AI ingests live on-chain data from Base mainnet, runs it through multi-agent AI pipelines, and delivers actionable intelligence, wallet flows, contract risk, and real-time alerts in one unified view.

Focus

Base Mainnet

Blocks, transactions, and wallet signals from mainnet, unified in one live intelligence layer.

Engine

Multi-Agent AI

Parallel agents parse, correlate, and score on-chain activity before you act on it.

Output

Actionable Intel

Flows, contract risk, and alerts surfaced in clear, operator-ready readouts.

Platform Capabilities

01

Wallet & flow tracking

Trace wallet clusters, inflows, and suspicious routing across addresses in real time. See how capital moves between entities before price or liquidity reacts on-chain.

02

Contract risk scoring

AI-assisted scans flag honeypots, proxy patterns, and abnormal bytecode before you interact. Each address gets a clear risk tier you can trust at a glance.

03

Live alert engine

MEV activity, liquidity shifts, and anomalies are surfaced before they escalate. Configure watchlists and get intel when mainnet behavior deviates from the norm.

04

On-chain news feed

A signal map and live news stream turn raw mainnet events into readable headlines: whale transfers to perp venues, bridge flows, liquidations, and DEX rotations. Each item includes AI context and optional trade ideas so you can connect on-chain activity to your next move.
